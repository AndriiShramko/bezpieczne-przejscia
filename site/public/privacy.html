Site & demonstrator

  • Without your consent the site sets no tracking cookies, runs no analytics in the browser and does not connect to Google. Google Analytics runs only after you click “Accept” — details below.
  • Analysis runs on a public camera; faces and plates are blurred, frames are not stored, no embeddings are computed. Only counters and blurred event snapshots are saved for verification — no personal data in the output.
  • Verification votes (confirm/refute) are used to compute accuracy. With each vote the server stores the IP address only so that one address gets one vote per event (the vote can be changed); the IP is not published and not sent to Google.
  • Contact form: we process the data (name, e-mail, message) to answer your inquiry and to be able to contact you again later about working together — GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)/(f); technical notification via Telegram. We keep contact data without a fixed time limit, until you ask us to delete it or object; just write to zmei116@gmail.com. Rights: access, rectification, erasure, objection, complaint to the Polish DPA.

Cookies & Google Analytics 4

  • Only with consent. When you click “Accept” in the banner we start Google Analytics 4 (provider: Google Ireland Ltd., Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland). Before consent and after “Reject” the site sends no data to Google.
  • Purpose and legal basis: aggregate usage statistics — page views, scrolling, clicks on buttons and links, votes, shares, language switch, clip playback, use of the form (never its content). Basis: your consent (GDPR Art. 6(1)(a) and the cookie rules of the ePrivacy law). Name, e-mail and message never reach Google; Google Signals and ad personalisation are off.
  • Cookies: _ga and _ga_4561KTT50X (a random browser and session identifier), for the domain patrol.flyreelstudio.eu only, valid for up to 400 days. Event data are kept in Google Analytics for 14 months and then deleted automatically.
  • Transfer outside the EEA: Google may also process data in the USA — under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework and standard contractual clauses.
  • Withdrawing consent: at any time via the “Cookie settings” link in the footer of every page → “Reject”. We then switch Google Analytics off, delete the _ga* cookies and remember your choice. Withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of earlier processing.
  • Browser storage (localStorage, not cookies): your consent choice (sa_consent), the language (bp_lang) and your votes for highlighting the buttons (bp_voted2) — they stay in your browser and are not sent to Google.
  • Server logs: like any web server, the server keeps technical access logs (incl. IP address, time, page, browser) for security and aggregate visit statistics (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)); the logs are rotated and overwritten automatically.

No output may be treated as an accusation of, or evidence for, any offence.
